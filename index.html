ABOUT

what this place is

The GG Site is a small hub for games — a quiet corner of the internet where a handful of little projects live side by side.

Every game here keeps its own folder inside /games. The site reads a short README.md from each folder for its description, so adding a new game is as simple as dropping in a new folder and updating the manifest.

Nothing here is trying to be a platform. It's just a launcher.
